# Snake

A nostalgic retro game: the classic snake, made for the Mudita Kompakt E Ink phone. Fully offline, with no score, timer or streaks, and no permissions.

[![ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/img/githubbutton_sm.svg)](https://ko-fi.com/J4V82A4UVG)

## What it does
- **Four ways to play:** Slow, Medium, Fast, and Step by step, where the snake moves one cell each time you tap a direction.
- **Controls made for the screen:** tap the field above, below, left or right of the centre, or swipe. Two taps ahead are remembered.
- **Nothing starts by itself:** the snake waits for your first tap, also after a pause.
- **Walls your way:** hitting a wall ends the game, or switch on "Pass through walls" in Settings.
- **Saved games:** each way of playing keeps its own game. It pauses and saves itself when you leave or the screen turns off.
- **Ten languages:** Croatian, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish and Czech.
- **Made for E Ink:** pure black and white, no animation, and only the head and tail change on each step.

## Privacy
Fully offline: the app has no internet permission, asks for no phone permissions and uses no Google services.

## Install
Download the latest APK from [Releases](../../releases/latest) and install it on your Kompakt with Mudita Center.
